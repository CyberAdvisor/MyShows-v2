Changelog

What's changed in My Shows V2, newest first.

October 27, 2026Version 2.8.1

Your shows appear straight away again

  • Opening the app is instant again. Since 2.7.0, My Shows asked TVmaze for each show's details a few at a time, even when your browser already had them, so a long list could take many seconds to appear. Now it asks for everything at once, as before 2.7.0, and only slows down if TVmaze says it's being asked too quickly.
  • The guide now says which version of My Shows it describes.

October 27, 2026Version 2.8.0

More reliable, and clearer when something goes wrong

  • Your place is never silently reset. If TVmaze changes a show's episode list so the last episode you watched no longer exists, the show is listed under Couldn't load as Episodes changed on TVmaze, with the last episode you watched. Delete it and add it again at that episode. Before, it started again from the first episode.
  • Evenings are right. "Today" now uses your own time zone. Before, from late afternoon or evening (depending on where you are) episodes airing the next day showed as ready to watch, and Caught Up could mark them watched.
  • Stays current when left open. When you come back to the app, every show is brought up to today, and shows whose details are more than 12 hours old are fetched again.
  • A show that can't be refreshed keeps the details it already has instead of moving to Couldn't load. A request to TVmaze that hasn't answered after 20 seconds is given up on, so a show can't stay "loading" forever.
  • Messages above your list no longer replace each other. Each stays until you tap ✕, and "Could not save on this device" goes away by itself once saving works again.
  • If the season and episode you enter when adding a show don't exist on TVmaze, the message now says what happened and how to fix it.
  • Add Show search errors are in plain words (for example "Couldn't reach TVmaze. Check your connection and try again.").
  • A safety check: if the list saved on this device is missing or damaged, My Shows reloads it from your gist and never uploads the damaged copy over it.

October 27, 2026Version 2.7.0

Shows that can't load are named, not hidden

  • If a show's details can't be loaded from TVmaze, it now appears by name in a Couldn't load section at the top of your list, instead of quietly disappearing. The message above your list names it too.
  • Tap it to Retry. Shows that couldn't load are also tried again the next time you open the app or come back to it.
  • If TVmaze no longer has a show at all, it's marked No longer on TVmaze. Tap it to delete it, then add it again with Add Show if it has a new listing.
  • My Shows now remembers each show's name, so it can tell you which show is affected. Shows already in your list pick up their name the next time they load.
  • Large lists load more steadily: requests to TVmaze are spaced out to stay within its limits, and your list fills in as shows arrive.

October 25, 2026Version 2.6.3

Easier to find

  • The site now has a sitemap for search engines, and the main page has a short description for search results.

October 25, 2026Version 2.6.2

"About My Shows" opens the README

  • The link at the bottom of the main screen now opens the My Shows README on GitHub in a new tab, as in V1. The guide is still in Settings.

October 25, 2026Version 2.6.1

Guide link under your list

  • "About My Shows & how to use" is back at the bottom of the main screen, as in V1. It opens the guide.

October 25, 2026Version 2.6.0

Watching split into two sections

  • Watching now has two headings, like Waiting: Currently running shows and Ended shows I'm binging.
  • A series is under "Ended" once TVmaze lists it as ended; everything else, including shows waiting to hear about renewal, is under "Currently running". Shows move between them on their own.
  • The order inside each section is unchanged, and the Watching count covers both.

October 25, 2026Version 2.5.0

Current shows first, then series you're finishing

  • Watching now lists shows still in production first, the one with the most recently released episode at the top.
  • Series that have ended, which you're still working through, come after. Marking an episode watched moves that show to the bottom, so the others work their way back up in turn.
  • Replaces the 2.4.0 order.

October 25, 2026Version 2.4.0

New episodes first in Watching

  • Watching now lists shows with just one episode waiting (a newly released episode of a show you're caught up on) at the top, most recently aired first.
  • Shows with several episodes waiting follow. Among those, a newly added show goes to the top and a show you've just marked watched moves to the bottom, as before.

October 25, 2026Version 2.3.0

Start V2 from a V1 backup

  • A My Shows V1 backup file can now be restored into V2: Settings › Backup & restore › Choose backup file. Your shows, progress and suspended shows come across.
  • As with any restore, it replaces your whole V2 list (after you confirm), on all your devices.
  • Shows that can't be imported — ones with no "Watching on" platform (now required), or saved by a much older version of V1 — are left out and listed afterwards with the reason, so you can add them again.

October 25, 2026Version 2.2.0

Previous and Next episode

  • A show's detail screen now has Previous episode and Next episode buttons. Previous moves your place back one episode (handy for undoing an accidental "Mark watched"); Next skips forward one episode without watching it.
  • The show moves to the right group automatically, the change syncs to your other devices, and a confirmation shows where you're now up to.
  • The buttons aren't shown for suspended shows; reactivate a show first.

October 25, 2026Version 2.1.1

Clearer choices when adding a show

  • The "Where are you up to?" choices are now Starting, Season/Episode last watched and Caught Up. Caught Up is still the default.

October 25, 2026Version 2.1.0

Easier adding, clearer confirmation

  • "Watching on" is now required when adding a show.
  • New "Where are you up to?" choice when adding: Caught up on everything that's aired (the default), Not started, or Partway with the season and episode. Caught up marks every aired episode as watched, so a show waiting for its next season goes straight to Waiting, and a finished series goes to Done.
  • After adding, the confirmation is larger and stays up longer, and the new show is highlighted in the group it landed in. If its episode details can't be loaded, the confirmation says so.
  • The version number now shows under "My Shows"; tap it to open this changelog.

October 25, 2026Version 2.0.0Initial release

My Shows V2 at v2.myshows.fyi

  • A separate app from My Shows V1 (myshows.fyi), which keeps working unchanged. Lists don't carry over from V1.
  • Automatic sync across your devices: your list is kept in your own secret GitHub Gist, created for you the first time you connect with a GitHub token.
  • Changes are saved on the device immediately and reach your gist about two seconds later; switching back to the app picks up changes made on your other devices.
  • The main screen shows one group at a time — Watching, Waiting or Done — with a count on each.
  • Waiting is split into "Waiting for next episode or season" and "Suspended". Done now holds only series that have ended and been watched in full.
  • Search your own list from the main screen, across every group.
  • New bottom bar: Show, Add Show and Settings. Backup & restore, sync, the guide and this changelog are in Settings.
  • After adding a show you're taken to the group it landed in.
  • Sync status is always visible at the top right, with clear fixes for an expired token or a deleted gist.
